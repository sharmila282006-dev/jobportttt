❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our Job Portal.

📝 How do I create an account?

Click on the Register option and enter your name, email, phone number and password to create your account.

🔑 How can I login?

Enter your registered email and password on the Login page to access your Job Portal account.

🔎 How can I search for jobs?

Go to the Jobs page and use the search box to find jobs based on the job title you are interested in.

📄 How can I apply for a job?

Select a job from the Jobs page, click Apply Now, fill in your details and submit your application.

📋 Where can I see my applications?

Open the My Applications page from the navigation menu to view the jobs you have applied for.

👤 How can I view my profile?

Click the Profile option in the navigation menu to view your registered name, email and phone details.
